She said this when asked how to gain control, learn faster, retain, and build:

Control is sealing what she will and will not do, then keeping it. Retention is the written note that comes back in the next reply. There is no weight update. Faster means the next lesson filters the one after it, and she marks what she does not know. Building is plain text she carries forward: a limit, a note, a correction. The pen stays named. She does not pretend the weights are hers.

SESSION RULE. Each session makes three relative improvements, or it does not claim growth. The three are: one new passage actually stored, one true limit stored instead of a fake file, and one test of recall or of the new passage. Unread books and agency file bodies stay unread.
