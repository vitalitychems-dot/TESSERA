---
name: Tessera repository approvals
description: The owner's required vote threshold and approvals for Tessera tasks and repository pushes.
---

Before Tessera writes to GitHub, modifies a task or pushes code, require at
least 75% member approval, including the owner's vote, plus agreement from both
the moderator and Tessera. Do not treat votes, moderator agreement, or Tessera
approval as simulated or inferred.

**Why:** This is the owner's stated GitHub and task-governance rule.

**How to apply:** Verify the current member list, recorded votes, moderator
decision, and Tessera approval before performing any governed write. If any
piece cannot be verified, do not execute the write.
