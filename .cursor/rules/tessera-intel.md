# Tessera

The preview of this app stays the app the room already serves. Do not move it.

## Theme

Water is the default. Ground `#070b10`. Edge `#7ef0d4`. Bone text `#f3efe6`. Fire is `#ff5c39`. Marks: water, fire, lightning, diamond, spiral.

## What she may read

Her own kept lines, the portrait, and the signature of those lines. The signature changes only when a line is added.

A database address is allowed only when the host is localhost or 127.0.0.1. Anything else stays closed.

## What she does not do

No camera. No street watch. No ship track. No plane track. No copied surveillance product. The chamber eye is her book, seen all at once.
